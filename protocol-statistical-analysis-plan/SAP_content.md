# The BE WELL study Black Raspberry Beverage Working to Prevent Lung Cancer

Statistical analysis plan

Trial registration NCT04267874 | Supplied protocol 2019C0089 version 6 dated 16 February 2022 | SAP revision dated 8 October 2026

This statistical analysis plan describes the feasibility, biomarker, microbiome and safety reporting for the BE WELL randomized crossover trial, using the analyses implemented for the manuscript. Nasal swab collection and associated nasal inflammatory transcript analyses were removed from the protocol during COVID-19 because the procedure was classified as aerosol generating and created major logistical barriers to trial implementation. Nasal RNA-seq and analyses requiring nasal gene-expression data are outside the final analysis scope. The SAP distinguishes verified manuscript implementations from information requiring clarification and is read with the final approved protocol and amendment record.

## 2 Background and objectives

Long term smokers have elevated lung cancer risk and chronic inflammation. Black raspberry (BRB) phytochemicals may alter microbial communities and inflammatory responses. This pilot evaluates acceptability of the intervention and repeated biospecimen collection and estimates biological responses for subsequent studies.

Primary feasibility objectives are recruitment and longitudinal biospecimen collection, with original protocol targets of at least 10% recruitment and 60% compliance and completion of at least 26 of 42 participants. Following removal of nasal sampling during COVID-19, the remaining scientific analyses reported for the manuscript concern stool microbial abundance/community characteristics, circulating inflammatory proteins and urinary polyphenol responses. Nasal inflammatory transcripts and gene–microbe analyses requiring those transcripts are excluded from the final analysis scope.

The original protocol also describes cellular immune phenotypes/function, T cell receptor diversity and immune senescence. This SAP does not specify new analyses of these endpoints; any final reported results and their generating code must be identified in the completed-trial record. No lung cancer incidence endpoint is specified.

## 3 Study methods

The trial is a single center randomized placebo controlled two sequence two period crossover study. Allocation is 1 to 1 to BRB first or placebo first, stratified by sex and current versus former smoking, using REDCap block randomization (section 5.1). Period 1 spans weeks 0–4, washout weeks 4–6, and period 2 weeks 6–10. Participants follow a controlled phenolic diet. Coded products mask participant treatment identity; section 8.4.2 states that study staff know product identity.

The manuscript reports two 170 mL servings per day during each four week intervention period. This schedule is used for the adherence description, consistent with protocol section 5.2. Actual dispensing records govern dose counts and exposure summaries.

The protocol sample size calculation is in section 3.1.1 and Figure 2: 26 completers provide 80% power for a 1.8 fold transcript change at coefficient of variation 0.3 with correction for 200 tests. At approximately 60% compliance, 42 participants are targeted for study visits. With estimated 56% loss between consent and first collection, 96 consents are anticipated. These are planning quantities, not observed enrollment. Replacements described in section 5.5 will be counted separately; every randomized participant remains in the disposition denominator.

The pilot uses a superiority framework for BRB versus placebo biomarker responses and a feasibility decision framework for recruitment and completion. The protocol describes a one sided 10% error and 90% power for feasibility versus 40% follow-up; other scientific tests will be two sided at 0.05.

No statistical efficacy interim analysis or alpha spending rule is specified. Section 10 requires ongoing safety and data quality review at monthly thoracic and quarterly lung screening meetings. Section 7.1 requires reporting grade 2 events and stopping after two similar grade 3 events for investigator and committee review.

Final analysis follows completion of week 10 follow-up or withdrawal, laboratory QC, data queries and a documented validated database freeze. Outcomes are analyzed together, with unavailable assays listed explicitly. Biospecimens are scheduled at weeks 0, 4, 6 and 10. Weeks 0 and 6 are period baselines; weeks 4 and 10 are period endpoints. Baseline counseling/screening occurs within four weeks before intervention.

## 4 Statistical principles and analysis populations

Scientific tests use nominal two sided P values with 0.05 as the reported threshold unless a specific panel documents another display criterion. The metabolite notebook calculates Benjamini–Hochberg (BH) adjusted P values across metabolite paired tests, but selects plotted metabolites and responder-score features using raw P < 0.05. The inspected cytokine mixed-model and taxon GLM notebooks report raw P values without a BH step. The original protocol refers to correction for 200 tests and the manuscript statistical paragraph states that differential-abundance P values receive BH adjustment; the final adjustment and hypothesis family require verification.

Recruitment summaries use retained screening and enrollment logs. The manuscript Table 1 describes two overlapping cohorts: all participants marked consented and those marked completed_all_visits in clinical_manuscript.rds; these are not randomized-sequence comparison groups. Scientific analyses use available assay-specific measurements matched to the participant/timepoint key, with complete pre/post pairs for paired tests and complete matrices/covariates for multivariate analyses. Cytokine mixed models use the available pre- and post-BRB records and repeat the fit within metabolite-defined responders. These are available-case and selected-subset analyses, not a verified complete intention-to-treat analysis. Safety includes participants with actual exposure.

Beverage adherence is summarized from diaries and returned containers, with the prescribed twice daily schedule and missing reports documented. Manuscript adherence and biospecimen-return summaries are reported among study completers; randomized-population feasibility is reported separately. Urinary polyphenol change is used as a biochemical response indicator and to define an exploratory responder subset. In metabolites_bewell.Rmd, metabolites with raw paired-test P < 0.05 are standardized by metabolite; each participant receives the mean standardized change, and a score at or above the sample median defines a responder. The final written responder file uses this median split, overriding the earlier two-cluster definition in the same notebook.

Protocol deviations include incorrect product, incomplete washout, out of window collection, prohibited supplements/probiotics or medications, smoking status change, and assay/sample handling failure. Summarize frequency and affected participants by sequence and period, distinguishing major deviations that compromise interpretation. The approved removal of nasal sampling is a protocol change rather than a participant-level missed collection.

## 5 Trial population and reporting

Eligibility follows section 4.1: ages 55–77, at least 30 pack years, current smoking or cessation within 15 years, willingness to follow the diet and supplement restrictions. Exclusions include ingredient allergy, relevant uncontrolled metabolic or malabsorptive illness, specified immunosuppressive/steroid/anticoagulant/probiotic use, active cancer treatment, pregnancy/lactation, planned smoking cessation, and nodules requiring further management. The approved eligibility screener governs ambiguous smoking wording.

Report screening counts and reasons for ineligibility and non-enrollment by recruitment source where retained, with an explicit unavailable category. The CONSORT flow reports approached, screened, eligible, consented, randomized by sequence, treatment initiation and completion in each period, washout, withdrawals and endpoint specific analyzed populations. Replacements are identified without deleting original participants.

Withdrawals distinguish stopping product from withdrawal of follow-up, with nominal week/period, elapsed time and reasons reported by sequence and treatment. Under the protocol withdrawal rules, participants withdrawing during an intervention may contribute stool and urine collections. Valid available measurements remain in their corresponding manuscript analysis populations.

Baseline Table 1 follows table1_baseline.R: summarize all consented participants and all-visit completers, with n and percentages for sex, race, age group, smoking status, HEI-2015 category and IPAQ category, and mean/SD for age and energy-residual EDIP/EDIH. Sex is Female/Male; race is White/African American/Other or unknown; ages are grouped as 55–66 and 67–79 years; HEPA active is relabeled Active, with Minimally active and Inactive retained. The stored HEI-2015 categories are Good (>80), Fair (51–79) and Poor (<50). Missing counts are added explicitly for reporting. These overlapping cohorts are descriptive; no between-cohort significance tests are performed.

## 6 Outcome definitions and crossover contrasts

Recruitment is the number agreeing to participate among unique approached individuals, with screened, eligible, consented and randomized counts also reported. Present n/N and whether the observed estimate meets the protocol 10% target. Completion is participants completing scheduled study visits divided by the documented randomized denominator; report n/N alongside the original 60% criterion and 26 of 42 planning target. If the actual denominator differs, identify it explicitly. Manuscript adherence/return summaries among completers are presented separately from recruitment and trial retention.

Biospecimen collection feasibility is reported as collected specimens divided by required scheduled specimens for each type and nominal visit, with assay usability and reasons for absent collections recorded separately. Apply the approved schedule in effect for each participant. Nasal specimens no longer required after the COVID-19 protocol amendment are not counted as missed collections or included in the required-specimen denominator. Distinguish collection rates among study completers, as reported in the manuscript, from full trial retention.

The protocol treatment-comparison concept is the difference between each participant’s BRB-period change and placebo-period change. The implemented manuscript analyses mainly evaluate pre- versus post-BRB responses; placebo measurements provide contextual summaries and separate within-placebo tests where coded. A pre/post BRB association is not the full crossover BRB-versus-placebo treatment contrast. In implemented models, post-BRB is the binary response and biomarker concentration or relative abundance is the predictor; effect estimates are log odds slopes rather than changes in concentration or abundance.

The original protocol included nasal inflammatory transcripts and stool microbial abundance. Nasal sampling and its associated analyses were removed during COVID-19 because nasal swabbing was classified as an aerosol-generating procedure and created major logistical barriers to trial implementation. The manuscript reports MetaPhlAn taxonomic relative abundances and circulating inflammatory proteins. Community comparisons use Bray–Curtis PERMANOVA and PCA; Simpson values are generated from the source notebook’s concatenated kingdom-through-species matrices.

Urine metabolites are analyzed as concentrations in ng/mL in the worksheet concentration ng mL, with post-minus-pre changes. Cytokine models use untransformed avg.res measurements from the processed table. Taxon models use the stored RelAbun scale without transformation; public MetaPhlAn rank tables contain percentages, so coefficients are per stored percentage-point unit unless the final processing record says otherwise. Standardized changes are used for heatmaps, PCA and polyphenol response scores. Assay units, censoring and vendor algorithm versions must be documented for any additional endpoint.

## 7 Scientific analysis methods

7.1 Urinary metabolites and biochemical response. Follow metabolites_bewell.Rmd. Read the concentration ng mL worksheet, retain metabolite rows with nonmissing 21-V2 values, convert sample columns to numeric, replace resulting missing numeric entries with zero as coded, and map visits to rP/tP/rB/tB using samples_long. For each metabolite, compare participant-matched rB and tB using a two sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test; the four-timepoint figure also requests a paired within-placebo comparison. Calculate BH adjusted P values across the BRB metabolite tests. Select display/responder-score metabolites using raw P < 0.05. Derive changes as tB minus rB and standardize each metabolite across participants for the heatmap and mean-change responder score.

7.2 Cytokine and inflammatory protein models. Follow cytokines_bewell.Rmd. Use averaged measurements for 14 analytes: ALCAM/CD166, CRP, CCL2, complement C9, cystatin C, IL-1β/IL-1F2, IL-10, IL-12/IL-23 p40, IL-6, MMP-2, S100A9, TNF-α, vWF A2 and IL-17F. Fit separately for each analyte logit Pr(post-BRB=1) = intercept + analyte slope × concentration + participant random intercept using lme4::glmer(..., family=binomial), restricted to preBRB/postBRB records. No treatment-period, sequence, sex or smoking adjustment appears in these fits. Repeat within subjects labeled Responder in the final polyphenol-response file. Report slope, standard error, Wald z/P, modeled record/participant counts and fit diagnostics. A positive slope indicates an association with the post-BRB label, not a causal concentration-change estimate.

7.3 Cytokine multivariate analyses. Create participant post-minus-pre BRB cytokine changes and column-wise z scores for the heatmap. The all-polyphenol analysis constructs a complete-case participant-by-metabolite change matrix, performs scaled PCA, and uses metabolite PC1 as UrinePoly_all. Complete cytokine and covariate records enter vegan::cca, which is canonical correspondence analysis. Retain rows with positive summed standardized cytokine changes, then shift the matrix by subtracting its minimum and adding 10^-6 when nonpositive values occur. Fit cytokine matrix ~ smoking + race/ethnicity + age + gender + urinary-polyphenol PC1. Use sequential term permutation tests (by="term"), 9999 permutations and seed 123; report F statistics. For the polyphenol high/low display, split PC1 into two ntile groups, perform scaled PCA of the cytokine matrix and PERMANOVA on Euclidean distances with adonis2 defaults (999 permutations), seed 123.

7.4 Microbial differential abundance and summaries. The active source conrad_bewell_data_for_mimic.Rmd fits a separate binomial glm for every feature in each kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus and species table: post-BRB label (1 for tB, 0 for rB/rP/tP) ~ untransformed relative abundance. This is univariable logistic regression without a participant random effect; estimates are log odds slopes. No prevalence >20% filter, top-40 restriction, covariate adjustment or BH step appears in the active model path. Representative taxa are displayed in four-timepoint boxplots; phylum bars use sample-normalized abundances with the top seven median-abundance phyla and Other. A separate saved object, model_stats_BW.rda containing model_res_BW, selects taxa for the microbiome-response grouping using raw P < 0.05.

7.5 Diversity and microbiome response grouping. In alpha_diversity_check.Rmd, concatenate the seven taxonomy-rank tables and compute vegan Simpson diversity; compare rB versus tB and rP versus tP with default t.test, which is a two sided unpaired Welch test. Figure4C&D-bewell.Rmd selects taxa from the saved model table at raw P < 0.05, sums their stored relative abundance per participant and BRB visit, and classifies a response when (tB + 10^-6)/(rB + 10^-6) is at least 2. Standardized PCA uses all baseline rB taxa in eligible participants. Compare response groups with Bray–Curtis adonis2, using the original abundance matrix and default 999 unrestricted participant permutations. This is a baseline-group association, not a randomized treatment test.

7.6 Physical activity and microbial response. The activity PERMANOVA path classifies participants as Active when IPAQ is HEPA active or METwalking + METmoderate + METvig + METmins is at least 5000; otherwise Inactive, retaining documented nonmissing activity records. Construct Bray–Curtis distances from post-BRB abundances, fit an unadjusted Activity comparison, then Pre_PC1 + Activity with marginal term tests and 9999 permutations. Test group dispersion with betadisper/permutest (9999). The Lachnospiraceae barplot computes (tB−rB)^2/2 and labels BRB Effective at 40.5 or greater, equivalent to absolute abundance change at least 9 stored units. This criterion includes decreases as well as increases and displays proportions by activity group.

7.7 Removal of nasal sampling during COVID-19. Nasal swab collection was removed from the protocol after its classification as an aerosol-generating procedure caused major logistical problems for trial implementation. Consequently, nasal inflammatory transcript testing, nasal inflammatory gene/pathway analyses and nasal gene–microbe integration were removed from the final analysis scope. The methods in sections 7.1–7.6 describe the analyses used for the manuscript; no new count-model, functional pathway, nonlinear-linking, full-crossover sensitivity or alternative multiplicity analysis is added.

## 8 Assumptions sensitivity analyses and missing data

Use the implemented analysis-specific records: complete pre/post pairs for metabolite tests; available pre/post BRB records for single-analyte mixed models; complete cytokine/metabolite/covariate matrices for PCA/CCA/PERMANOVA; and available taxon-table rows for GLMs. Report counts and exclusions separately for each assay, subgroup and multivariate stage. Table 1 consented/completer cohorts do not establish the assay-specific analyzed populations. No multiple imputation or last-observation-carried-forward step appears in the inspected manuscript notebooks.

Cytokine preprocessing follows processing_cytokines_avg-res.Rmd: remove standards/background; flag concentration values outside 1.5 IQR fences and unusually high replicate CV; exclude a replicate only when both flags apply; average remaining replicates by sample/analyte. The coded CV IQR is computed within analyte, while the subsequent CV quantile is pooled after ungrouping. Strip inequality markers from some concentration entries, and convert NaN to zero in the fourth batch. Append IL17F ELISA measurements. Retain an audit of these rules, numeric conversions and exclusions.

Check convergence, singularity, complete/quasi separation and influence in logistic models; report failed fits as unavailable. Inspect feature variance and missingness before ordination and report model rank, dispersion tests and permutation settings. Any repair needed to execute a notebook is recorded separately, with its effect on final results. 

The implemented manuscript models do not estimate period or sequence terms or perform a documented carryover test. The two-week washout is a design assumption. The inspected manuscript analyses are reported with those limits; this SAP does not introduce new carryover, period-1, full-crossover or per-protocol sensitivity analyses.

Outcome-derived metabolite responder scores, significant-taxon response groups and physical-activity response subsets are exploratory. Their nominal tests and selected displays do not establish treatment-by-subgroup interaction, mediation, independent validation or causality. The SAP records the manuscript implementations and adds no alternative-model or missing-data analysis.

## 9 Additional analyses and harms

Additional manuscript analyses include polyphenol-response subgroup cytokine models, cytokine change ordination with demographic and urinary polyphenol covariates, baseline microbiome response groups, and physical-activity association with Lachnospiraceae responses. They use outcome-derived feature/group selections and are exploratory. A vanillic acid–CRP association and additional univariate PERMANOVA comparisons are described in the manuscript but their complete generating code is not present in manuscript/scripts.

Harms are assessed using CTCAE version 5.0 at visits and phone calls (sections 7 and 10). Summarize exposed participants, duration and total events, participants with any event, serious events, grade at least 2 and grade at least 3, maximum grade per participant, relatedness, expectedness, discontinuations and deaths by actual treatment and sequence. Recurrent episodes count as multiple events but participant incidence counts once per category. Washout events are shown separately; no unrecorded event is assumed absent.

Safety reporting is descriptive, with n/N and the event summaries documented in the completed-trial record. Treatment-specific denominators include only participants with documented exposure and completed ascertainment; missing ascertainment is reported. Investigators adjudicate events around crossover/washout from the source record, rather than automatically attributing them from nominal visit week.

Analyses use R. manuscript/r-packages.txt records R 4.5.1 and the available package versions, including lme4 1.1.37, vegan 2.7.3, dplyr 1.1.4, readxl 1.5.0.1 and tableone 0.13.2. Scripts for unperformed proposed analyses have been removed.

Use a validated deidentified analysis freeze with unique participant/sample identifiers and the randomization and assay manifests. Source queries, sample selection, QC, derivations and exclusions are logged. Preserve the approved SAP and amendments alongside the analysis code and output index in the statistical master file. The protocol, consent records and approvals remain in the trial master file.

Outputs include recruitment summaries, consented/completer Table 1, assay and subset sample counts, metabolite paired tests and BH values, responder definitions, cytokine log odds coefficients, taxon model tables, Simpson summaries/tests, PCA/PERMANOVA and activity-response summaries. Full outputs are retained even when a figure highlights nominally selected features. Protocol analyses without an implemented result are listed separately; candidate reruns are not substituted for final submitted values.
